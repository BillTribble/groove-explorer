## 1. Tresillo / 3-3-2 Family (the foundational cell)

| **Kizomba** | F | `...x..x....x..x.` | **Identical top to Dembow** — slower, smoother |

## Suggested UI Consolidation

For a cleaner selector, these could be single entries with sub-variants:

1. **Dembow** → [Dembow, Kizomba, Malfuf, Dominican dembow]
2. **Tresillo** → [Tresillo, Habanera, Son, Rumba, Bossa, Highlife, Gahu, 111, Dancehall, Bomba, Azonto, Pon pon, Tarraxinha, Batida, Zanku]
3. **Cinquillo** → [Cinquillo, Calypso, Zouk, Soca]
4. **Samba** → [Samba, Afoxé, Baile funk]
5. **Afrobeat** → [Afrobeat, Afrobeats]
6. **Gqom** → [Gqom, 3-step, Amapiano, Afro house, Afro house 12/8, Kwaito, Bacardi, Shangaan]
7. **Kuduro** → [Kuduro, Kuduro shuffle, Kuduro triplet, Ndombolo, Coupé-décalé, Batida, Funaná, Singeli]
8. **12/8 Bell** → [Standard bell, Bikutsi, Salegy, Maloya, Gnawa, Afro house triplet]
9. **North African** → [Maqsum, Saidi, Malfuf]

The "Timelines & claves" region already captures the Tresillo family well. The main redundancy is **Dembow/Kizomba/Malfuf** and **Funaná/Singeli** being separate entries with identical patterns.
