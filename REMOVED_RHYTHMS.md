# Removed rhythms

Removed 2026-11-14 because their data held no pattern: every part was a steady pulse (or a triple gallop cut off to fit a 16-step row),
and no pattern could be supplied with confidence. Each is still in git history (`js/rhythms.js` before this change).
To bring one back, research its real timeline or lead pattern first.

Frevo, Ndombolo (cavacha) and Batuque were restored the same day from published notation.
Mongombi, Intore, Chakacha and Coco were restored once parts could loop on their own length: their gallop now plays whole
(three steps against the 16-step bar). That gallop is the library's original, not a sourced transcription; a web search found
no published pattern for any of the four, nor for Nyamaropa.
